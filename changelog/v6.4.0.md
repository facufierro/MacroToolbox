# MacroToolbox v6.4.0

## Changes

- Fixed copying behaviors that use trigger-dependent actions into the same profile.
- Fixed repeated behavior copies hiding one another and targeting the wrong copy when editing, deleting, or reordering.
- Added a Duplicate action for variables that preserves their type and value while creating a separate copy.
- Added app subfolders with switches and configurable shortcuts to activate or deactivate their profiles together.
- Placed sortable subfolders above profiles, inserted new subfolders at the top, and added a settings button to app folders.
- Added dragging profiles into subfolders, between subfolders, and back to their app folder.
- Added dragging behavior rows onto profiles to move them with their references, and enabled whole-row dragging for behaviors and profiles.
- Added moving whole overlays and individual parts onto sidebar profiles, plus dragging parts between overlays, while preserving their settings and references.
- Simplified Wait for to a parameter-free step that uses the next action, including mouse press/release settings, and automatically converted saved input waits without losing their conditions.
- Added multiple-overlay selection to Start, Restart, and Stop overlay behaviors, preserving selections when copying, importing, or deleting overlays.
- Fixed local rebuilds being treated as released versions when selecting the development release.
